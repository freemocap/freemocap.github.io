# Get started with FreeMoCap

FreeMoCap turns ordinary webcams into a 3D motion capture rig. This page is
the short version: four steps, no branching, from nothing installed to a
skeleton moving on your screen.

1. **[Install FreeMoCap](/start/install)**, either with `pip` or with the
   packaged installer for your OS.
2. **[Make your first recording](/start/first-recording)**, one camera, a
   few minutes, so you can confirm the whole pipeline works before adding
   more cameras.
3. **[See what you made](/start/see-your-results)**, find the output files
   and confirm the recording tracked cleanly.
4. **[Where to go next](/start/where-next)**, once you have a working
   recording, decide whether you need multiple cameras, better calibration,
   or want to go straight to analyzing the data or bringing it into Blender.

If you already know your way around Python environments and just want the
commands, the short version is at the top of the
[install page](/start/install). If you get stuck anywhere in this path,
[installation troubleshooting](/guides/installation-troubleshooting) and
[calibration troubleshooting](/guides/calibration-troubleshooting) cover
the most common problems, and the [Discord](https://discord.gg/XpRQJnqZxf)
`#help-requests` channel covers everything else.
