# Get started with FreeMoCap

FreeMoCap turns ordinary webcams into a 3D motion capture system. Four steps take you from
nothing installed to a skeleton moving on your screen.

1. **[Install FreeMoCap](/start/install).**
2. **[Make your first recording](/start/first-recording)** with one camera, to check that
   everything works.
3. **[See what you made](/start/see-your-results).**
4. **[Decide where to go next](/start/where-next):** more cameras, better calibration,
   analyzing your data, or Blender.

Stuck? See [fix an installation problem](/guides/installation-troubleshooting) or
[fix a calibration problem](/guides/calibration-troubleshooting), or ask in `#help-requests` on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/XpRQJnqZxf).
